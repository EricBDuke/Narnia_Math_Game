A PROFESSIONAL FRAMEWORK FOR RESPONSIBLE AI USE

Before using AI during an interior design project, designers can apply five questions:

1. APPROPRIATE

Should AI be used for this task?

Consider whether the task involves confidential, culturally sensitive, safety-critical or otherwise sensitive information.

2. PROTECT

What information am I providing to the AI system?

Remove or protect confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information where required.

3. QUESTION

What assumptions or errors might be contained in the output?

Examine the response critically rather than accepting it because it appears convincing.

4. VERIFY

What information needs to be checked independently?

Verify technical, regulatory, product, environmental and other consequential information using appropriate authoritative sources.

5. DISCLOSE

Could someone misunderstand how this material was produced or what it represents?

Clearly identify AI-assisted or conceptual material where disclosure is appropriate, particularly when realistic imagery could be mistaken for a resolved design.
