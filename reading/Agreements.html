AGREEMENTS

When working as an interior designer, it is crucial to have agreements in place that are to be signed by the client at important stages throughout the design process. This includes getting a client signature on all purchase orders, with note that any custom or special order is non-refundable or may be subject to a restocking fee.

You should also have a lawyer look over all of your agreements to ensure they are legally binding in case of disputes, and for larger projects, hire a lawyer to prepare a formal contract on your behalf.

Try not to use a contract you find off the internet. Your business is special and unique; don’t try to protect yourself with someone else’s contract.

LETTER OF ACCEPTANCE

The letter of acceptance, also known as a service agreement, is used to confirm your fee and the services you intend to provide.

This letter will form a binding agreement between you and the client, and it is important that you clearly list all of the details of the project to ensure there are no gray areas.

This should be done once they have agreed that you’re the right person for the job, a budget has been decided on and your fee structure approved.

The content of the letter will need to be amended to suit the nature of the project and if a quotation has been approved prior to the letter being written.

Have the client sign it to confirm they have read and understood the terms of the agreement.

The letter of acceptance will suit most applications, but for more complex projects, it is better to employ a lawyer to prepare a contract or a set of terms and conditions that are included with the documentation.

The following page includes a sample letter of acceptance. Of course, you don’t have to follow it to the word, but it will give you some idea of what needs to be included.

The following page includes an example letter of acceptance that shows the type of detail to include for a design package.

Thank you for choosing [Your Business Name] to undertake the design work on your house at [Client’s Address].

You have selected our [insert design package name] for the price of $X, and for this package, [Your Business Name] will carry out the following services:

  • Specify the type of service that the package is made up. For example: These can include: room styling, color selection, source and specify products, kitchen design, bathroom design or a full redesign.
  • List the room type that the package fee will include. For example: bedroom, living area, kitchen, dining, bathroom or laundry.
  • Be sure to include a quantity for each of these so there are no misunderstandings. For example: bedroom room styling package to include source and specification of furniture, artwork and accessories for one room only.
  • List the items that the client will receive as part of the package. For example: sample boards, technical drawings, fixture fitting and equipment schedule, 3D models, floor plans, reflected ceiling plans.
  • Specify the number of client meetings and what they will involve. For example: Two client meetings, one to present a design concept followed by a second meeting to present the final design.
  • Quantify the number of design revisions the client can make as part of the package, if any. For example: This package includes one revision to the design proposal before the final design is agreed.

Detail any additional fees that are applicable to work that falls outside of the package:

  • Any additional work outside of the package will incur an extra charge and detail what this is. For example: Additional sample boards will be charged as an additional $X per board.
  • Detail the extra fees incurred to revise the design. For example: Changes to the design made after the final design approval will incur an extra fee calculated at $X per hour to produce the revised design.

Include payment amount and terms. For example: [insert design package name] to be invoiced and paid in full before work on the project commences.

AGREEMENT

Separate to the letter of acceptance, a more detailed agreement can be written to outline all components of the project clearly. This can be supplied with the letter of acceptance to expand on the details of the project(s), the responsibilities of you and the client and explain the consequences if these are not met.

Some examples of information that can be included in the agreement are as follows and are to be used as a guide. You will need employ the services of a lawyer to draw up your standard legal contract to complete each project.

This Interior Design Agreement is between legal company name (Designer) and client’s name (Client) with respect to the interior design services to be rendered for full project address (Project) by Designer for Client dated date.

Designer and Client agree as follows:

SCOPE

Designer shall develop interior furnishing specifications that may include color consultancy, fabrics, lighting and furnishings as required. Designer’s services do not include trade services, landscape design or architecture.

Designer shall consult with other professionals, such as lighting consultants, architects and others. Client acknowledges that the project deadlines are subject to the vagaries of the marketplace and the performance of third party suppliers.

Page 48

DESIGN FEES

(Detail the fee structure clearly; provided is an example of an hourly rate fee structure).

Designer shall be compensated on an hourly basis at the rate of $X per hour. All hours or partial hours Designer is working on and/or for the Project will be considered design fees. Hourly charges will be invoiced to Client in 10-hour increments and are payable by Client upon receipt of invoice. Upon signing this Agreement, Designer shall receive a non-refundable initial advance of $X, which constitutes the minimum fee due Designer for Design Services. The advance will be credited against hourly fees otherwise payable by Client to Designer for Design Services at completion of project. All Design Fees are non-refundable.

PURCHASING

Purchasing is billed at a rate of $X per hour. Full deposit is required for each item, and only upon receipt of the deposit will Designer place order. Designer shall arrange delivery and installation of Designer-purchased furnishings and other items purchased on behalf of the Client for the Project. If available, Client will receive 50% of Designer discount and Designer will receive 50%.

When practical, Designer will present specification to Client for Client to purchase direct from vendor. Client is wholly responsible for all items purchased by the Client.

REFUNDS & CANCELLATIONS

Once purchased, most items cannot be returned or canceled. Therefore, requests to do so will be assessed on a per-item basis with no guarantee of return or cancellation. Custom items cannot be canceled or returned. Requests for returns and cancellations will be billed hourly. Design Fees and reimbursable expenses are non-refundable, even when associated with a return or cancellation.

NO PRICE GUARANTEE

Designer cannot guarantee the prices of merchandise, interior installation or other services not performed by Designer. Vendor pricing is subject to change and out of the control of Designer.

REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

Client agrees to reimburse Designer for all out-of-pocket expenses actually incurred by Designer in relation to the Project, including but not limited to, renderings, drafting services, postage and handling, freight, delivery and storage costs. Client shall reimburse Designer for all travel, lodging and meal expenses incurred by Designer and Designer’s staff (where necessary) in connection with the Project.

PAYMENT

Invoices for time billing and reimbursable expenses are due within 14 days. Invoices more than 30 days past due will accrue a 10% late fee per month, and all work will cease after the first month until the invoice is paid. Designer shall be entitled to withhold delivery of any item purchased on behalf of Client should Client fail to make any payments due to Designer in a timely manner.

DRAWINGS

Designer’s drawings are conceptual in nature and are intended to set forth design intent; they are not to be used for architectural or engineering purposes. Designer services do not include modifications to structural, heating, air conditioning, plumbing, electrical, ventilation or other mechanical systems in the Project.

Designer shall be held harmless for relying on the accuracy of information provided by the Client. Project drawings and documents cannot be used by Client for any purpose other than completion of Project by Designer as laid out in this Agreement.

TRADES & CONSULTANTS

If Project requires trades and/or consultants to perform work based on Designer’s concepts, Client will enter in a contract directly with each trade and/or consultant.

Designer provides no warranty, guarantee, certification or responsibility for the performance, quality or timely completion of any work performed or materials installed by trades nor their agents or employees. Designer shall cooperate with and observe Consultants for the purpose of general conformity of the design plan but is not responsible for their oversight.

PERMITS

Client is responsible for permits and or any and all approvals and compliance required by any governmental agency.

INSURANCE

Client is required to have insurance coverage for all furnishings and materials during handling, moving, storage and installation.

Client is responsible for ensuring that their insurance coverage is sufficient as per this Agreement. Designer cannot be held responsible for inadequate insurance coverage.

PHOTOGRAPHS & PUBLICITY

Client agrees to allow Designer and/or Designer’s representatives to photograph Project during all stages of Design Services including when Project is complete. Photographs will be used for business purposes including but not limited to press, publications, online, social media, marketing, advertising and print.

Designer will not disclose the address or Client’s name without prior consent. Costs of photographs and publicity are the responsibility of the Designer. Additionally, if Client or Client’s agents document the Project, Designer shall be given credit as the Designer if documentation is released publicly.

TERMINATION

Designer or Client can terminate this agreement by notifying the other party in writing.

Client will be responsible for any outstanding reimbursable charges and hourly fees. All in-process proposals and orders will be completed by Designer and delivered to Client at hourly rate.

If a balance remains in Client’s account, the amount will be refunded less any outstanding reimbursable charges or design fees. Client agrees to take no action which is intended or would reasonably be expected to harm the Designer’s reputation or which would reasonably be expected to lead to unwanted or unfavorable publicity to the Designer.

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Neither the Designer, Designer’s Consultants or their agents nor employees shall be jointly or individually liable to the Owner for an amount in excess of the proceeds of the available professional liability insurance coverage.

TERMS

The parties agree that the terms of this Agreement may be changed only in writing, which is to be signed by both parties and that no oral changes or waivers are permitted.
