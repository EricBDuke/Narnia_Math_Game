PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE PRINCIPLE

The responsible use of AI can be summarised by one principle:

The more consequential the design decision, the greater the level of human oversight and verification required. Using AI to generate ten possible names for a design concept presents relatively little risk.

Using AI to determine whether a proposed stair, accessible bathroom, fire exit or material specification complies with regulatory requirements presents considerably greater risk.

Professional designers must recognize this difference.

AI literacy in interior design therefore involves more than learning how to generate impressive images or effective prompts. It requires designers to understand the limitations, risks and consequences of using AI and to maintain professional judgement throughout the design process.

The question for the future interior designer is not simply: “Can I use AI to do this?”

It is also: “Should I use AI for this, how will I verify the result, and am I prepared to take professional responsibility for the final decision?”
